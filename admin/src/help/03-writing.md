# Writing

Posts open as a writing canvas with a title and body. Template pages show content
fields beside a preview. Your agent handles the site structure and design; use the
CMS to refine the words and images.

## Arrange your workspace

**Details** shows or hides the post’s URL, summary, image and publishing settings. Its sections open independently,
and Lanza remembers your choices. **Focus** hides the navigation and details while
keeping save controls available. **Format** opens formatting controls on demand. Choose **Exit focus** to bring them back.

Text fields grow as you write. Longer titles wrap, and dates, image controls and
paragraph fields use the space they need.

## Editing a landing page

The page name, URL and language sit above your workspace. **Content** opens the first
section immediately. Choose another section from the selector, or click text in the
preview to jump to its field. Change existing text, links and images here; your agent
handles adding sections and changing the design.

**Page details** contains search appearance and metadata. **Changes** holds the page's
review. The preview stays beside your fields on wide screens and sits above them on
small screens. Animations are paused in this preview so text stays steady as you edit.

## Formatting

Use the formatting toolbar, or type **`/`** on an empty line to insert a heading,
list, quote, table, callout, image or embed. Standard shortcuts work, including
⌘/Ctrl-B for bold and ⌘/Ctrl-I for italic. Tables and multi-paragraph callouts survive
opening and saving.

If a loaded document contains formatting the editor cannot preserve, saving is
blocked and a message explains why. Ask your agent to adapt that content first.

## Saving and recovery

Posts and pages **autosave after a two-second pause** once they have a title. Save
still works whenever you want it. Saving stores a draft version; publishing is a
separate action. Typing during a save stays unsaved until the next save completes.

Unfinished writing gets a recovery copy in the current browser tab when session
storage is available. After refreshing, choose **Restore writing** or **Keep saved
version**. Review restored writing, then save to resume autosaving. This recovery
copy is not a backup across devices or after closing the tab.

If a save fails, autosave pauses and your writing remains in the editor. If an agent
or another editor changed the stored entry, Lanza refuses to overwrite that newer
version. Reload to review it; the recovery copy retains your unfinished writing.

Changing an existing URL requires an explicit **Save**. Reverting a field in the
change review also pauses autosave, so you can inspect the result before saving it.

## Preparing to publish

**Draft** keeps an entry out of the live site. **Ready** includes it when the owner
publishes the site's pending changes. Setting Ready and saving does not publish the
site. See *Publishing & deploy* for the final step.
